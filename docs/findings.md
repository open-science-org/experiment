# Findings so far

Last updated: 2026-11-07. Plain summaries; each links to its full result.

## Simulation

- **Sharing at once is the big saving.** OSO reaches the targets for 3.5–5.7× less money than traditional research, mostly because nobody repeats work that is already done.
- **Flow-back matters when agents choose what to share.** With the rules deciding, it changes little. When agents choose, they share 24% of their ideas at α 1 and 66% at α 0.2.
- **The knowledge reward matters once money follows earnings** (α 0.8: 1.8 of 2 targets with it, 0.4 without). [Result](../results/knowledge-reward-by-step/summary.md)

## AI agents on Lean problems

- **Lemmas help weak models:** DeepSeek proves 4 of 16 targets alone, 12 of 16 with the lemmas. Strong models need no help. [Panel](../results/pilot/panel-2026-10-19/summary.md)
- **OSO against traditional:** in 5 paired races OSO reached the hard targets in every run, for about half the money. Traditional proved the same groundwork twice 20 times a run. [Race](../results/community/race-cheap/summary.md)
- **Part B, labs cannot stop (20 runs per setup):** no clear difference in targets proved. But the more value flowed back, the more lemmas labs proved (7.2 → 13.8 a run) and the more they built on each other. [Results](ai-agent-results.md)
- **B5 check, fair prompts (running):** after 5 runs per setup, OSO α 0.2 proved more lemmas (19.6 vs 5.2) and B5 more often (3 of 5 vs 1 of 5). Too few runs to settle.

**Not yet tested:** AI agents choosing what to publish (the core OSO claim), other models, and real mathematics designed by others. See the roadmap.
