# Hard graph (10 × 10, long sequences): α × β

**Question:** On a hard idea graph (10 levels × 10, 2.5 parents, sequences of 4 bits growing by 3, bounties from level 5), how do α and β decide how far a society gets?

**Answer:** Best overall: OSO (α = 0.5, β = 1). Against traditional, clearly better on hardest level, targets solved, bounty value, ROI and waste. (6 measures; a clear difference is more than 2.6 standard errors.)

- hardest level: 8.70 against traditional 1.65, clearly better.
- targets solved: 6.03 against traditional 0.35, clearly better.
- bounty value: 1341 against traditional 24, clearly better.
- ROI: 0.22 against traditional 0.00, clearly better.
- money to the main level: best α = 1, β = 0.75, 2388.
- waste: 0.17 against traditional 0.54, clearly better.
