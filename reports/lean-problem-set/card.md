# The Lean problem set

**Question:** For the same money, does OSO get further than traditional research in the Lean problem set?

**Answer:** OSO ahead: level 7.0 against 4.4 of 7; targets 8.0 against 4.8 of 8; ROI 2.01 against 0.31.

![The Lean problem set](figures/frontier.svg)

| rules | hardest level | targets solved | cost to level 5 | ROI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional | 4.4 of 7 | 4.8 of 8 | 303 | 0.31 (118 for 394) |
| OSO | 7.0 of 7 | 8.0 of 8 | 125 | 2.01 (460 for 243) |

**Design** (fixed before the run): compared the six rule settings, an α sweep, the parameters one by one, costly and fallible sharing · held the same: the field, 12 agents and a budget of 400 · measure: hardest level reached, higher is better · 100 runs per setting.

**Caveats:**
- A simulation with guessed parameters: it shows how the rules play out if the field works as described.
- OSO's lead as the market-set parameters vary: 0.1 to 2.6 levels.
- Theorem names are hidden; the tree is the route of our own reference proofs, and agents may find others.
