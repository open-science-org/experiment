# A solid-state battery

**Question:** For the same money, does OSO get further than traditional research in a solid-state battery?

**Answer:** OSO ahead: level 5.0 against 2.0 of 5; targets 1.9 against 0.6 of 2.

| rules | hardest level | targets solved | cost to level 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional | 2.0 of 5 | 0.6 of 2 | 222 |
| OSO | 5.0 of 5 | 1.9 of 2 | 136 |

**Design** (fixed before the run): compared the six rule settings, an α sweep, the parameters one by one, costly and fallible sharing · held the same: the field, 12 agents and a budget of 240 · measure: hardest level reached, higher is better · 100 runs per setting.

**Caveats:**
- A simulation with guessed parameters: it shows how the rules play out if the field works as described.
- OSO's lead as the market-set parameters vary: 0.1 to 3.0 levels.
- The structure is a simplified sketch, and the success chances follow the default model by depth; neither is from the battery literature.
