# An abstract research field

**Question:** For the same money, does OSO get further than traditional research in an abstract research field?

**Answer:** OSO ahead: level 7.9 against 5.7 of 8; targets 6.9 against 4.2 of 7.

![An abstract research field](figures/frontier.svg)

| rules | hardest level | targets solved | cost to level 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional | 5.7 of 8 | 4.2 of 7 | 957 |
| OSO | 7.9 of 8 | 6.9 of 7 | 485 |

**Design** (fixed before the run): compared the six rule settings, an α sweep, the parameters one by one, costly and fallible sharing · held the same: the field, 12 agents and a budget of 1200 · measure: hardest level reached, higher is better · 100 runs per setting.

**Caveats:**
- A simulation with guessed parameters: it shows how the rules play out if the field works as described.
- OSO's lead as the market-set parameters vary: 0.3 to 1.9 levels.
