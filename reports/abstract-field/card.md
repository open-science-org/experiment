# An abstract research field

**Question:** For the same money, does OSO get further than traditional research in an abstract research field?

**Answer:** OSO ahead: level 8.0 against 3.7 of 8; targets 3.6 against 0.7 of 4; ROI 2.70 against 0.36.

![An abstract research field](figures/frontier.svg)

| rules | hardest level | targets solved | cost to level 8 | ROI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional | 3.7 of 8 | 0.7 of 4 | 686 | 0.36 (343 for 947) |
| OSO | 8.0 of 8 | 3.6 of 4 | 401 | 2.70 (1859 for 747) |

**Design** (fixed before the run): compared the six rule settings, an α sweep, the parameters one by one, costly and fallible sharing · held the same: the field, 12 agents and a budget of 950 · measure: hardest level reached, higher is better · 100 runs per setting.

**Caveats:**
- A simulation with guessed parameters: it shows how the rules play out if the field works as described.
- OSO's lead as the market-set parameters vary: 0.0 to 5.8 levels.
