# Example: a deep, wide field

**Question:** For the same money, does OSO get further than traditional research in Example: a deep, wide field?

**Answer:** OSO ahead: level 8.0 against 0.2 of 8; targets 3.7 against 0.0 of 4; ROI 3.24 against 0.01.

![Example: a deep, wide field](figures/frontier.svg)

| rules | hardest level | targets solved | cost to level 8 | ROI |

| Traditional | 0.2 of 8 | 0.0 of 4 | 570 | 0.01 (10 for 757) |
| OSO | 8.0 of 8 | 3.7 of 4 | 338 | 3.24 (1874 for 613) |

**Design** (fixed before the run): compared the six rule settings, an α sweep, the parameters one by one, costly and fallible sharing · held the same: the field, 12 agents and a budget of 760 · measure: hardest level reached, higher is better · 100 runs per setting.

**Caveats:**
- A simulation with guessed parameters: it shows how the rules play out if the field works as described.
- OSO's lead as the market-set parameters vary: 4.1 to 7.7 levels.
